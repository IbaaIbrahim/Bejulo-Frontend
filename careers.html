Full-time

Who we are: bejulo GmbH

bejulo GmbH is a company with over 13 years of experience in the development, engineering, construction and operation of utility-scale ground-mounted PV systems and BESS. With currently 20 employees, we operate internationally with a focus on project development in Germany. On our path to becoming an IPP, we also hold several assets in our own portfolio or together with partners.

What you can expect:

  • A forward-looking workplace in a dynamic company with flat hierarchies, a pleasant working atmosphere, and a collaborative team
  • A position of high responsibility
  • A varied and engaging field of tasks

What we look for in you:

  • Strong teamwork and interpersonal skills
  • Independent working style and high reliability
  • Excellent communication skills
  • Willingness to travel (approx. 65%) including intercultural competence

Your responsibilities include:

  • Technical management and supervision of solar and BESS projects across all phases (planning, construction, commissioning, handover, and operational phase)
  • Technical optimization of the plant regarding local and customer requirements
  • Supporting project management with regard to the economic viability of utilized components
  • Plant engineering and design from initial layout in the tender phase through detailed execution planning to “As-Built” documentation for PV and BESS
  • Coordination and implementation of grid connections with on-site and normative requirements
  • Preparation of bills of quantities / tender specifications
  • Briefing and coordination of site management and subcontractors on the respective project (construction site experience is an advantage)

Professional skills & qualifications:

  • Solid experience in the field of photovoltaic systems (ideally also proficient with CAD, PVsyst)
  • Proficient in standard Office applications
  • Very good written and spoken English skills
